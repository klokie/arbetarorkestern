Arbetarorkestern plays the closing party of Klimatfestival för 17 at Bagarmossens Folkets Hus. We're on at **7:30–8:15 PM** with music about a little love, politics and everything in between.

**Free entry, no registration.** Food and drinks are sold at the bar. Dancing encouraged.

## The evening's music

Doors from 6:00 PM, music 6:30–10:15 PM (possibly later).

- **Många bäckar små** — folk songs and poems
- **Arbetarorkestern+** — a little love, politics and everything in between
- **Nämndemanz** — rock and pop covers
- **Karaff** — original indie/pop
